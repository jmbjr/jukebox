h. LETTER AND NUMBER SERIES SWITCH
ADJUSTMENT

The letter series switch may be adjusted
with the latch solenoid plunger at rest position.
Loosen the screws (fig. 3, item 6), set the long
blade of the series switch to zero clearance with
the fiber stud (item 7). The contacts should open
1/32" with any letter button depressed and should
close with 1/32" overtravel with the button re-
leased.
