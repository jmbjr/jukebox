SELECTOR SWITCH ADJUSTMENT

The selector switch assembly may be removed
from the cabinet for servicing. Open the lower front
door by releasing the two latch levers located near
the ends of the lower fluorescent lamp. Remove
the four hex head screws that secure the selector
switch assembly mounting brackets to the inner sides
of the cabinet. Lift the selector switch assembly
straight up.

a. CONNECTOR LINK PLATE ADJUSTMENT

Should the connector link plate require ad-
justment, readjust the entire assembly through the
following sequences.

(1) Loosen the screws (fig. 1, item 3) and
(fig, 2, items 6 and 8),

(2) Fully depress a number button, Position
the lever (fig. 1, item 11) in a vertical position and
tighten the screws (fig, 1, item 3). Following the
same procedure, adjust the letter connector link
plate.

c. STOP BRACKET ADJUSTMENT -
LATCH SOLENOID

(1) The stop bracket (fig. 3, item 3) should
be adjusted with the release lever (item 4) in its
normal rest position. Loosen the adjusting screws
(item 2) and move the stop bracket to provide 1/64"
clearance between the square stud (item 11) and the
latch pawl as shown at item 1.

(2) Back the adjusting screw (item 10) out
to provide 1/64" clearance between the square stud
(item 9) and the trip lever (item 5) as shown at
item 8.

¢. LATCH ADJUSTMENT

The latch adjusting screws (fig. 2, items 6
and 8) were loosened to perform the link adjustments
(see paragraph a).

(1) To adjust the number latch lever, en-
ergize the latch solenoid (fig. 2, item 9) by estab-
lishing a credit on the Playrak, then manually hold
a number button fully depressed. Move the number
latch pawl (fig. 4, item 5) to allow the trip lever to
rest against the stop tab (item 7). The latch lever
(item 5) should rest firmly against the square stud
of the number trip lever (item 4). Maintain this
setting as the screw (item 6) is tightened. Release
the number button by depressing the button release,
Check each number button for proper latching and
selector switch contact engagement.

(2) To adjust the letter latch lever: (fig. 4,
item 1), follow the same procedure outlined in (1).
