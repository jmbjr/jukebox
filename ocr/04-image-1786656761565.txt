SPEAKER CONNECTIONS

A terminal board is provided at the ourput of the
Model 548 Dual Channel Solid State Amplifier to
which both phonograph speaker lines are connected.
Each channel has four low impedance output termi-
nals labeled A, B, C, and D. In addition, one termi-
nal labeled G is the common ground for both. The
phonograph speakers are normally connected at the
factory to terminals A on each channel and the com-
mon ground G, When installing remote auxiliary
speakers, it may be desirable to reduce the phono-
graph output while maintaining a relatively high out-
put at the remote speakers. To do this, it is only
necessary to move the phonograph speaker leads
from the A terminals to B, C, or D, whichever pro-
vides the desired level. CV or 500 ohm speakers
(speakers equipped with a matching transformer)
may be connected to the Model 548 Amplifier to op-
erate stereophonic or monophonic as illustrated,

In making this check, be sure to use the same
channel. Also, it is advisable to disconnect the
phono speakers.

NOTE: The installation of line matching trans-
formers is recommended for installations requiring
long runs of wire or when auxiliary speakers may be
operated at various sound levels. All speakers
should be in phase for best results. One terminal of
Wurlitzer Auxiliary Speakers is marked G. This
terminal of low impedance speakers (those without
matching transformers) should be connected to the
G terminal of the Model 548 Amplifier identified with
a yellow dot. The G of CV or 500 ohm speakers
(with matching transformers) should be connected to
the CY terminal of the Model 545 Amplifier identified
with a blue dot.

CAUTION |

DO NOT CONNECT CV OR 500 OHM SPEAK-
ER LINES TO THE G TERMINAL OR CHASSIS
GROUND OF THE MODEL 548 AMPLIFIER. TO
DO SO WILL RESULT IN REDUCED OUTPUT.

transformer connected to500 ohm tap is 5 at full out -
put, or 10 if connected to 1000 ohm tap. For reduced
output more speakers may be used.

(those less matching transformers) to CV terminals.
If impedance of speaker line is unknown try it on the
A and G low impedance terminals and then on the CV
terminals. If loudness is about the same, connect the
speaker line to A, B, C, or Dand G, If loudness is
much greater on CV than on A&G, connect line to CY
terminals, Disconnect phono speakers for this test).

connected to the low impedance output terminals .

to one 8 ohm speaker. One 31/2 ohm speakers is
equivalent to two 8 ohm speakers in parallel.

The phonograph speakers may be considered as one 8
ohm speaker per channel. They may be moved to B,
C, or D for required level with respect to level of
auxiliary speakers.
