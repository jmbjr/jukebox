Model 548 Dual Channel Solid State Amplifier to
which both phonograph speaker lines are connected.

factory to terminals A on each channel and the com=

necessary to move the phonograph speaker leads
from the A terminals to B, GC, or D, whichever pro-
vides the desired level. CV or 500 ohm speakers

Maximum number of wall speakers with matching
transformer connected to500 ohm tap is 5 at full out -
Put, or 10 if connected to 1000 ohm tap. For reduced
output more speakers may be used.

CAUTION Do not connect low impedance speakers
(those less matching transformers) to CY terminals.
If impedance of speaker line is unknown try it on the
A and G low impedance terminals and then on the CV
terminals. If loudness is about the same, connect the
speaker line to A, B, C, or D and G. If loudness is
much greater on CV than on A &G, connect line to CV
terminals. Disconnect phono speakers for this test).

Maximum number of 8 ohm speakers which may be
connected to the low impedance output terminals .
Note: Two 16 ohm speakers in parallel are equivalent
to one 8 ohm speaker. One 31/2 ohm speakers is
equivalent to two 8 ohm speakers in parallel.

The phonograph speakers may be considered as one 8
ohm speaker per channel. They may be moved to B,
C, or D for required level with respect to level of
auxiliary speakers.
