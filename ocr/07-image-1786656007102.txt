g., DRIVER SOLENOID ADJUSTMENT

(1) The two driver solenoids shown in
figure 12, labeled L.H. and R.H., serve to turn the
rotating plate and rocker arm assembly to position
the tips of the rocker arms at the correct selector
pin as determined by the number button selected. To
check the adjustment, manually hold the R.H. driver
solenoid plunger bottomed in the solenoid. The
coupling spring of the plug and spring assembly
(item 10) should stretch 1/16" while the stop bracket
{item 4) on the rotating plate and rocker arm assem-
bly is held firmly against the extreme right hand stop
(item 6). Adjustment may be accomplished by loosen-
ing two screws in the solenoid mounting bracket, one
of which is shown at item 8. It is important to main-
tain the straight-in-line position between the solenoid
and plunger and there must be no bind on the rotating
plate or its operating linkage.

h. ELECTRIC SELECTOR CENTERING

Centering of the 200 selector assembly must
be carefully done whenever the unit is removed from
the changer mechanism. The assembly is held in
position by two mounting screws (fig. 14, item 6) and
by the supporr plate (fig. 15, item 4) at the rear of
the changer.

(1) To remove the electric selector assem-
bly from the changer mechanism, disconnect the
mute plug from the amplifier, the chassis plug from
the junction box and the changer motor plug from the
junction box. The cables wired to the plugs discon-
nected above should be removed from the cable clips
on the support plate and placed on top of the chassis
mounting plate where they will be out of the work
area, Disconnect the four plugs on the bottom of the
electric selector. Manually rotate the record car-
rier so that the selector crank arms are approxi-
mately over A-O and L-O selections, Remove the
two front mounting cap screws (fig. 14, item 6) first,
which allows the electric selector to rest on the front
guide brackets (items 1 and 5). Loosen the rear
upper mounting screws (fig. 15, item 2). Grasp the
rear support plate with one hand below the screws
and remove both screws. Lower the support bracket
and the rear edge of the electric selector far enough
to clear the rotary contact wipers on the bottom of
the main shaft. With the other hand supporting the
electric selector from underneath, slowly move the
assembly down and to the rear, carefully removing it
from the cabinet.
