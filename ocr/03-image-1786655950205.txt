e. FINAL LATCH SOLENOID ADJUSTMENT

(1) Loosen the latch solenoid adjusting
screws (fig. 2, item 2). Manually hold the plunger
(item 1) firmly bottomed in the solenoid. Move the
solenoid to provide 5/64" maximum clearance he-
tween the tab (item 7) on the release lever and the
number trip lever (item 5).

(2) The plunger travel should not exceed
3/16". Check the release lever action for mechani-
cal bind of the plunger within the solenoid. Realign
the latch solenoid with the plunger if necessary but
maintain the adjustment as outlined above.
