c. REVERSE SWITCH ADJUSTMENT

The reverse switch (fig. 9, item 6) should
be checked after any adjustment of the forward stop
screw. Follow the same procedure used in adjusting
the start switch except turn the reverse switch ad-
justing screw (item 5) in two and one half turns. This
provides 3/32" overtravel.

d. BACK STOP SCREW ADJUSTMENT

The number quadrant (fig. 9, item 4) is held
against the back stop screw (item 2). in its normal
rest position by the retracting spring (item 3). The
following method may be used to adjust the back stop
screw.

The number quadrant must be in its normal
rest position.

Turn the back stop screw (fig. 9, item 2) in,
driving the number quadrant (item 4) forward until
the reverse switch (item 6) actuates. Turn the back
stop screw out until the reverse switch resets plus
two and one half turns for overtravel.
