# Troubleshooting knowledge

## Reported symptom

Selection buttons do not appear to select a record, while manual actuation of an unidentified rear mechanism can initiate successful operation.

## Manual fault tree: credit exists but selection is unavailable

Section F page 3F lists the following causes when credits are established, the Select lamp does not light and selections cannot be made:

1. Key switch open
2. Selector series switches open
3. 8 A DC fuse blown
4. Dirty relay contacts: RY-4 in the 200-selection model (RY-2 in the 100-selection model), or RY-12 as shown by the manual
5. Top Tunes jumper plug missing from the junction box
6. Defective latch solenoid
7. Normally closed button-release switch open or mechanically binding

## If the Select lamp lights but buttons do not latch

Inspect the keyboard/latch assembly before disturbing selector alignment:

- latch-solenoid plunger motion and binding
- control-switch gap and overtravel
- letter- and number-latch switch gap and overtravel
- letter and number series-switch continuity
- button-release switch operation
- wiring and plugs between keyboard and junction box

The manual gives specific mechanical clearances on pages 2B–3B. Do not approximate or bend contacts without checking those pages.

## If buttons latch but no selector pin releases

The likely boundary moves downstream:

- RY-2/RY-4 operation and contacts
- selection motor forward/reverse operation
- start and reverse switches
- driver and index solenoids
- letter and number selector coils
- selector plate/rocker alignment
- stuck latch pin
- No. 303 current-limiting lamp behavior

## If moving a selector pin manually starts scanning

That observation would support a fault before pin registration and would provide evidence that the wobble/override switch and much of the changer sequence are functional. Record the exact pin and movement before drawing this conclusion.

## Safe measurement strategy

1. Start with power removed: continuity, coil resistance, fuse continuity, connector seating and mechanical freedom.
2. Compare readings with the manual and equivalent coils/contacts where appropriate.
3. Use energized voltage measurements only when the manual identifies the procedure and the operator can safely work around exposed mains voltage.
4. Do not trust OCR for a terminal, wire number, voltage or contact state; inspect the page image.
